## 10. Verdict

**Pass as presented — insufficient disclosure.**

*Screening output only, not investment advice.*

**Reasoning**
- The core return story can't be underwritten. Nothing shows how 18% is built, whether it is net, or over what hold, and most office essentials in `01` are absent.
- This is not a finding that the deal is bad. A deep-basis Class A building can be a real opportunity, and no flag here proves otherwise.
- But the three selling points don't support the conclusion. "Class A" is not "trophy in a resilient market," "85% leased" is not occupied, and a discount to a pre-reset price is not evidence of value.
- `GEN-19` is fired, and the debt timeline (`GEN-09`, `EQUITY-07`) gives the deal about two years before a financing event.

**Biggest swing factor:** in-place NOI at the 2028 maturity, after dark space rolls. It determines whether the loan refinances, whether the LP faces a capital call, and what the exit is worth. Physical occupancy and the lease schedule are the two facts that move it most.

**Re-screen list.** Send these and the deal can get a merits verdict:
1. Physical occupancy and submarket sublease and reabsorption data, with the market named and trophy vs. A class stated.
2. Rent roll, T-12 and lease expiration schedule.
3. Complete debt terms: amount, rate, cap, extensions, and the 2028 plan.
4. Hold, net-to-LP IRR, distribution schedule, and the split between cash flow and exit.
5. Unlevered return, plus a downside case with flat rents, higher exit cap and no refinance.
6. Full fee schedule and waterfall.
7. Sponsor identity, co-investment, and realized net-to-LP results.

**What would have to be true to pursue it**
- Physical occupancy is near the leased figure, or the underwriting already assumes dark space leaves.
- The return holds at a flat or higher exit cap.
- The maturity is covered by extensions that can actually be met, or by a plan that survives a shut refi market.
- The figure the LP is shown is net, and it still justifies office risk after fees.
- The sponsor has real co-investment and realized results.

**Who it could suit, if those hold:** an LP who accepts High-tier risk (`01`), wants no income reliability, can leave capital locked up past the stated hold, can absorb a capital call or a loss of principal, and holds this as a small position. It does not suit an LP who needs income or liquidity.
